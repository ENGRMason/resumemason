UV24 moulded carbon side panels

The side panels were designed using SolidWorks surfacing derived from chassis geometry, held at a 0.1 in offset from the frame. The work involved creating a positive mould and producing panels through wet layup woven carbon with vacuum bagging, resulting in lightweight, dimensionally accurate components compliant with 2024 FSAE rules.

UV25 replacement bodywork mould

Two weeks from competition and a $500 budget, after the original mould was destroyed. The alternative was laser-cut profiles spaced 2 in apart, mated to a profiled spine base. A 3D-printed nose piece handled the compound curvature, and foam reinforcement took the vacuum pressure during layup.

The rebuilt mould reproduced the profile of the original machined mould, survived layup and vacuum with no damage, and came in at roughly 75 percent less cost and time.

Design philosophy

The job is converting aerodynamic intent into parts that hold their shape under load. Attachment strategy gets most of the attention, because that is where student aero packages usually fail: not in the surface, but in the bracket.
