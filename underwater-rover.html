Underwater Navigation Robot for Autonomous Search and Repair

TypeAutonomous RoverDateApril 2023

A team-built rover designed to autonomously locate thermal sources and place objects on them, simulating real underwater maintenance scenarios: finding a heat signature on a submerged structure and delivering a component to it with no operator in the loop.

Key figures
SensingLow-resolution thermal camera
NavigationThermal gradient following
ManipulationServo-actuated placement mechanism at fixed standoff
ValidationDry-lab, heated targets

Thermal navigation

The rover uses a low-resolution thermal camera to generate coarse temperature imagery rather than single-point readings. That lets it follow thermal gradients toward a source instead of executing a blind search pattern. Once the source is localised, the rover holds a fixed standoff distance while a servo-actuated mechanism deposits the target object.

Test conditions

Testing was done in dry-lab conditions with heated targets. That isolates the autonomy and manipulation problem from the waterproofing and buoyancy work a fielded version would require.
