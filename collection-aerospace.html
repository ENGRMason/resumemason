Aerospace and Rockets

Aerospace work grounded in hands-on building: composite airframes, propulsion testing, recovery systems, and flight telemetry. Most of it came through UVic Rocketry, contributing across structures, composites, and fuel and combustion on a competition rocket that flew to 9,340 ft at Launch Canada 2023, alongside an independently built Raspberry Pi telemetry system for capturing and transmitting flight data in real time.

Disciplines and tooling

Carbon fiber layup, Composite fabrication, Static thrust testing, Python, Raspberry Pi, LoRa telemetry, Sensor integration
