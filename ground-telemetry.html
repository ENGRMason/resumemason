Aerospace and Rockets

Ground Telemetry System for Small Scale SS Engines

TypePersonalDateSummer 2023LocationCalgary, AB

A dual-component telemetry architecture for model rockets. The airborne segment records flight data locally while simultaneously broadcasting it; the ground segment receives and visualises the stream instantly. The goal was to stop treating a flight as a single number recovered afterward and start seeing the whole profile as it happens.

Airborne package

A BMP388 supplies barometric altitude and an LSM303 provides acceleration and orientation. A Raspberry Pi does the processing, with a PowerBoost 500 handling power regulation. The configuration writes to SD card concurrently with transmission, so the flight record survives even if radio contact fails. An onboard camera documents the flight, and everything mounts on 3D-printed fixtures.

Downlink

Communication runs over an RFM95W LoRa transceiver, chosen for long range at low power, optimising for connection reliability over transmission frequency. A packet protocol manages bidirectional acknowledgment between rocket and ground station.

The ground interface, built in Processing, graphs incoming data in real time with replay capability and scripted controls for session management. Post-flight metrics include peak altitude, velocity, and acceleration analysis.
